## Interpretation and packaging notes

- Attribution belongs in the README and this record. The running skill and complete guide contain direct writing instructions, examples, and useful writing-resource links, without original page, figure, or example labels.
- The original PDF has no established publication date in the supplied copy. Page numbers in this record refer to its printed numbering.
- Figures, example labels, footnotes, and page references describe provenance; their writing lessons are retained in the guide without making the agent read about how they were transferred.
- The source passage used in the reader's paraphrasing example mentions roughly 10% directly quoted material as a desirable ceiling in that passage's discussion. It is not established as a general TU/e grading requirement. The guide retains the actionable advice to limit verbatim note-taking and quotation, and to apply a numerical limit only when required by the assignment or publication.
- Resource URLs are the addresses supplied by the reader, not a guarantee that every address remains current. The guide tells the agent to verify resources when consulting them.
- Independently written examples preserve the source's teaching distinctions. Their hypothetical facts and citations are not evidence for use in actual academic work.
- Additional agent operating rules preserve requested task scope, prevent fabricated evidence, distinguish source verification from citation review, and prevent unsupported approval claims. They are implementation rules, not purported university policy.

## Exclusions

Only non-instructional presentation is omitted: the cover, contents navigation, repeated headers/footers, branding, and the motivational literary quotation. Motivational discussion's writing lesson is retained in §1. The suspense anecdote's reader-comprehension lesson is retained in §7. Original illustrative subject matter and data are not reproduced as factual evidence; their writing lessons are retained as mapped above. No writing recommendation is excluded merely because it is basic, prescriptive, or already familiar to an AI model.

## Maintenance acceptance scenarios

These scenarios support a manual content audit; they are not a claim that automated behavioral tests or an independent agent evaluation have run.

| Scenario | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Broad draft/revision/review request | Read the complete guide, including its style and source sections, before acting; do not rely on the entrypoint summary |
| Claim supported by a credible source about a different outcome | Identify the relevance failure, independently of source reliability |
| Claim and evidence with an obvious-seeming link | Require explicit reasoning, including the extent of support |
| Missing fact between two sentences | Identify the absent premise; do not repair with “therefore” alone or invent the fact |
| Clear relation but missing signal | Use a matching transition without changing the proposition |
| Standard argumentative paragraph | Opening arguable claim, evidence/reasoning, meaningful conclusion or transition; do not impose the model on every paragraph |
| Reviewer requested feedback only | Give located issues and remedies, without replacing the entire draft |
| Grammar-only request | Limit edits to the requested language scope despite reading the full guide |
| Informal style under TU/e defaults | Apply each specific style convention, including contractions, direct questions, FANBOYS, verb specificity, quantities, hedging, concision |
| Publication permits researcher “we” | Preserve the source's disciplinary exception; reject undefined general-public “we” under the default |
| Long source quotation | Recognize block/indented formatting and appropriate attribution; consider paraphrase in science/technology |
| Citation plus synonym-swapped source wording | Do not accept it as adequate paraphrase solely because it has a citation |
| Independent wording exaggerating a tentative finding | Flag source-fidelity failure despite lexical originality |
| Note-taking example mentions 10% quotation | Retain advice to limit transcription without claiming an institutional percentage requirement |
| Missing underlying sources | Distinguish citation-format review from source verification; never invent bibliographic facts |
